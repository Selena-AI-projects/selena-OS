# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

AI Visibility serves local businesses, hospitality teams, service companies,
founders and agencies that need to understand how their brand appears in AI
answers and what public evidence should be improved. Customers use the private
workspace to create projects, confirm the business context, review evidence and
work through grounded recommendations.

## Product Purpose

The product measures AI visibility with disclosed evidence, keeps Visitor View
separate from API View and converts findings into an actionable improvement
plan. Success means a customer can move from the Selena Systems website into a
recognisable client workspace without encountering Elmo internals, hidden spend
or unexplained technical identifiers.

## Positioning

AI Visibility records every measurement in an Evidence Ledger and ties
recommendations to observable evidence. It does not collapse every AI system
into one opaque score or promise guaranteed rankings.

## Operating Context

- `selenasystems.com` is the public marketing and free-readiness surface.
- `app.selenasystems.com` is the authenticated customer workspace.
- Visitor View covers ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.
- API View covers Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and Grok with web search off by
  default.
- Public website readiness can run without connecting private customer accounts.
- Paid measurements require an approved order, fixed cardinality and provider
  budget caps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current locked catalog is Visitor Local at $49/month, Full AI Landscape at
$79/month, Expert Verified at $399 one-time and Growth 90 Days at $2,490 with
manual approval. Online checkout, live payments, recurring maintenance and real
provider calls remain off until their separate owner gates and production
acceptance are complete. Google Places is excluded from the MVP. Connected
Analytics is optional and requires explicit account authorisation.

## Brand Commitments

AI Visibility is a product line inside Selena Systems, not a separate
company. The voice is clear, practical and evidence-first, without hype, fake
urgency or unverifiable claims. The approved seller for the current product
documents is PT Izi Jiza Bali, Indonesia.

## Evidence on Hand

- A public website-readiness check and sample report exist in the Selena Systems
  website repository.
- RC6 v1.2 was accepted in staging for this application.
- The Usha pilot includes a deterministic 240-row Evidence Ledger and client PDF.
- No public customer testimonials or general performance claims are approved.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before interpretation.
2. Visitor View and API View remain visibly separate.
3. No measurement or spend before an explicit approved boundary.
4. Internal IDs and operational controls stay out of the customer experience.
5. Automated findings state their limits; Expert Verified requires human QC.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must meet WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation and 44×44 px
minimum touch targets. English and Russian product routes must remain equivalent
in meaning and commercial scope.
